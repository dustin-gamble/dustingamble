Mission Trade Study Companion

November 6, 2025

  • Experiments
  • Mission Engineering
  • Tools

A lightweight analysis companion to compare mission concepts before committing to detailed design.

Problem / context

Mission concept discussions often start with strong intuition but weak comparability across options. Teams can spend significant time debating assumptions rather than evaluating a structured set of tradeoffs.

Approach

The companion tool concept was designed to capture mission assumptions, compute comparable scenario outputs, and present side-by-side trade views in a concise format suitable for technical reviews. The emphasis was speed, clarity, and transparent assumptions.

Outcome / lessons

The approach helped teams converge faster on which concepts deserved deeper investment. A key lesson was that early trade-study tools do not need high complexity; they need shared structure and clear decision criteria.
