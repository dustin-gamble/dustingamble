Professional Bio

Dustin Gamble is an aerospace engineer focused on autonomous systems, advanced UAV design, and AI-enabled engineering workflows. His work centers on rapidly transforming new ideas into practical flight systems, simulation tools, and engineering capabilities that accelerate innovation across aerospace programs.

Over the course of his career, Dustin has contributed to the design, development, and demonstration of multiple unmanned aerial systems spanning long-endurance fixed-wing aircraft, experimental autonomy platforms, rapid-prototype vehicles, and advanced mission concepts. A highlight was leading the effort that achieved a 39-hour endurance flight world record with the Stalker VXE unmanned aircraft.

He is the founder of the Drone Innovation Garage (DIG), an internal rapid-innovation environment focused on accelerating autonomous system development through experimentation, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and fast prototype iteration. Through this work he has mentored engineers and helped teams move quickly from concept to flight hardware while maintaining engineering rigor.

A significant portion of Dustin's recent work involves building engineering tools that combine simulation, data visualization, autonomy frameworks, and AI assistance. His philosophy is that engineering productivity increasingly depends on the thoughtful integration of AI with traditional analytical methods, domain expertise, and experimental validation.

Looking forward, he is especially interested in the convergence of autonomous flight, distributed sensing, AI-driven engineering workflows, and modular vehicle architectures.
