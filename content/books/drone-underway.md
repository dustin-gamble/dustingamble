## About the book

The same method, on the water.

[Drone Field Guide](https://gamble.systems/drone-field-guide/) gave the method for small aircraft: find the one or two requirements that size the machine, run the loop, write the prediction before the test, read the log. *Drone Underway* takes it to the lake, the river and the harbour, for small boats that survey, sample and inspect.

A displacement hull has a speed its waterline sets and nothing you can buy moves; below it a pack lasts hours, above it minutes. Two thrusters steer and one drifts. Weed is the stall of the water. The mission begins at the beach and ends at the beach, and the beach is where boats are lost.

Thirty-six chapters, from reading a hull across the ramp to the second boat. Each opens with its design decision and closes with a rule of thumb, with a worked example between them. One 1.5 m survey catamaran is sized through the whole book and never built, so that every number comes from the method. Quick-reference cards for the bench and the shore, and the forms, blank, at the back.
