## About the book

The same method, on the ground.

[Drone Field Guide](https://gamble.systems/drone-field-guide/) gave the method for small aircraft: find the one or two requirements that size the machine, run the loop, write the prediction before the test, read the log. *Drone Overland* takes it to the lawn, the orchard and the trail, for rovers you can lift.

Traction is the budget and everything else spends it. The hill sizes the pack, not the distance. A wheel that spins has already lost. The map is flat and the ground is not.

Thirty-six chapters, from reading a chassis across a parking lot to the second rover. Each opens with its design decision and closes with a rule of thumb, with a worked example between them. One 25 kg rover is sized through the whole book and never built, so that every number comes from the method. Quick-reference cards for the bench and the field, and the forms, blank, at the back.
