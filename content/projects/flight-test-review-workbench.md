## Problem / context

Post-flight review can become a bottleneck when data, events, and observations are spread across disconnected files and notes. Teams lose time reconstructing what happened before they can decide what to change next.

## Approach

This experiment explored a timeline-centric review workbench that aligns telemetry channels, event markers, and analyst annotations in one view. The concept focused on reducing manual stitching effort and improving consistency in after-action reporting.

## Outcome / lessons

Early prototypes showed faster identification of event sequences and better handoffs between test and development teams. The main lesson is that reducing interpretation friction after each flight can significantly improve weekly learning velocity.
