## Problem / context

Long-endurance unmanned aircraft programs face a compounding challenge: aerodynamic and propulsion efficiency are necessary, but operational reliability and integration quality usually decide real mission duration. The objective in this effort was to support a flight profile that could sustain performance over an exceptionally long period while maintaining robust operational execution.

## Approach

The work emphasized whole-system discipline rather than a single subsystem optimization. Focus areas included mission planning, simulation-informed preparation, reliability-oriented test practices, and clear decision criteria through execution windows. The technical posture prioritized repeatability, conservative risk framing, and strong integration between engineering and operations teams.

## Outcome / lessons

The program publicly achieved a 39-hour endurance flight world record with the Stalker VXE. The main lesson is that endurance performance is a systems outcome, not an isolated design trick. Operational rigor, integration quality, and disciplined preflight/test workflows are as important as raw efficiency metrics.
