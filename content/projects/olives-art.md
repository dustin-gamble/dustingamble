## Problem / context

My daughter Olive wanted to sell her artwork to help with a school fundraiser. She needed a simple place online where friends and family could browse her pieces and chip in.

## Approach

I built her a small, friendly art-shop website — a clean gallery of her work with an easy way to buy and support the cause. Like most of my recent side projects, it came together fast with AI-assisted development, which let me focus on making it approachable and fun rather than fighting boilerplate.

## Outcome

A real website with her name on it that she is proud of — and a low-stakes, high-joy way to show her that the things she makes can live on the internet and reach people.
