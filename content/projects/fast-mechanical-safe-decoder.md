## Problem / context

Many lock mechanisms hide useful physical signal behavior behind difficult-to-observe interfaces. The goal for this project was to create a compact, quickly built device that could explore safe mechanism response patterns and speed up experimentation.

## Approach

The design emphasized direct mechanical interaction, repeatable adjustment, and low-complexity construction. Rather than over-optimizing early, the project favored quick build-test iterations and observation-driven tuning.

Follow-on work included a second iteration to improve speed and consistency.

## Outcome / lessons

The project demonstrated that hands-on mechanism experiments can generate practical insight quickly when instrumentation and iteration loops are simple. It also reinforced a recurring engineering theme: testability and setup time strongly influence how fast ideas improve.
