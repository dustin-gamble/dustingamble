## Cover page

Take It In transforms a flight into a cinematic, chaptered audio journey built around the route you are actually flying.
The idea is simple: enter a flight, download the story before takeoff, and experience the landscape below through narration, visuals, and map context while offline at altitude.

This page is a hosted copy of the public cover page for `takeitin.app` while the standalone service is paused.
It is only the product-facing overview.

## How it works

1. Enter your flight number and date.
2. Generate a route-aware journey with chapters, landmarks, scenic context, and narration.
3. Download the full package before takeoff.
4. Play the experience offline while flying.

## Designed for the window seat

- **Offline-first playback:** the full journey lives on the phone after download.
- **Audio-first narration:** each chapter is written for the route and paced for the flight.
- **Route and chapter map:** the app shows progress, upcoming chapter markers, and location context.
- **Window-side hints:** prompts such as "look left" or "look right" help connect the story to the view.
- **Scenic imagery:** visuals support the narration when the view is blocked by clouds or distance.

## The experience

Take It In is meant to make ordinary air travel feel less like waiting and more like noticing.
It treats the flight path as a story structure: deserts, mountains, rivers, cities, islands, and coastlines become chapters that unfold as the aircraft moves.

The app is especially tuned for travelers who love the window seat, families who want a calmer in-flight experience, and anyone who has looked down from an airplane and wondered what they were seeing.
