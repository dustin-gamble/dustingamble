## Problem / context

AI Fusion Book is a writing experiment built around the question of how AI can support long-form creation without flattening the voice or turning the work into generic output. The project treats the book itself as both content and process: a finished artifact, and a demonstration of an AI-assisted writing workflow.

## Approach

The book was developed as a web-published experience rather than a static file. AI was used to help explore structure, refine passages, accelerate drafting, and support iteration, while the overall direction and framing stayed human-led.

The project also fits a broader theme across this site:

- use AI to accelerate production
- keep human judgment on tone and intent
- publish in formats that are easy to share and evolve

## Outcome / lessons

The book reinforced that AI-assisted writing works best when it is treated as a collaborator inside a process, not as a substitute for authorship. The output becomes more useful when the workflow is intentional and the final form is something people can actually browse, share, and return to.
