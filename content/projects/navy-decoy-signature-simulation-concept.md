## Problem / context

Certain mission environments require systems that can represent complex operational signatures without deploying high-value assets. The challenge in this concept area was to explore how a compact autonomous platform could emulate key signature characteristics relevant to naval operational scenarios.

## Approach

The work focused on high-level concept framing, system architecture trade studies, and practical feasibility pathways for demonstration. Emphasis was placed on mission realism, controllability, and transition viability, while keeping scope bounded to publicly discussable dimensions.

## Outcome / lessons

The concept was developed and demonstrated at a high level, then transitioned internally for further pursuit. A major lesson was that signature-simulation effectiveness depends on integration quality between mission logic, platform constraints, and operational context, not on any single isolated feature.
